# wedge opportunity scan

## islets
- readiness score: 0.91
- evidence score: 0.878
- commercial score: 0.837
- standard pattern: slow-freezing via Dimethyl Sulfoxide, Sucrose, Ethylene Glycol
- pain point: Vitrification and slow-freezing remain confounded by species and endpoint differences.
- why optimization matters: A cleaner benchmark wedge maps to transplant and cell-banking workflows where protocol uncertainty still blocks standardization.
- recommended next move: Pressure-test "Additive-assisted islet recovery benchmark on a fixed base cryomix" as the next wedge-defining experiment.

## ovarian-tissue
- readiness score: 0.561
- evidence score: 0.637
- commercial score: 0.809

## hepatocytes

- evidence score: 0.543
- commercial score: 0.784
